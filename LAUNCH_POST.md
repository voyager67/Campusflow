# CampusFlow — Launch post

I built **CampusFlow**, an early prototype exploring a simpler classroom-attendance workflow.

The demo includes a faculty dashboard, short-lived rotating QR check-ins, a fictional student roster, duplicate check-in rejection, local SQLite storage and CSV reports. It runs locally and does not require a dedicated student app.

This is an honest first step—not a production system. The demo student selector is not authentication, QR scanning does not prove physical presence, and CampusFlow is not connected to or approved by SAKEC. Before any real pilot, institution-approved identity, authorization, security/privacy review and written approval would be essential.

I'm sharing the prototype to learn, validate the workflow and get constructive feedback on the problem, architecture and next steps.
